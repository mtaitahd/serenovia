The JSON is an adapted route database distributed under ODbL 1.0.
Relation ways were joined through shared nodes, and shortest paths selected
between Machame Gate, Machame Camp, Shira Cave Camp, Lava Tower, Barranco,
Karanga, Barafu, Stella Point, Uhuru Peak, Barafu, Mweka Camp and Mweka Gate.
Camp coordinates were snapped to the nearest mapped relation node.
The six-day option combines the Barranco–Karanga and Karanga–Barafu segments.
For itinerary illustration, not turn-by-turn navigation. Hotel transfers are
not part of the walking track. No route API is called by website visitors.
